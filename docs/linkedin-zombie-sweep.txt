My No Pipeline, No Problem entry: Compute Optimizer found nothing idle on my AWS account. What did it miss?

A Step Functions sweep of every enabled region that runs zombiescan's checks beside Compute Optimizer's idle recommendations, stores findings in DynamoDB and publishes a priced report to S3 and CloudFront.

What is in it:

- 🟢 17 regions swept in 19 seconds, 35 Lambda invocations, no errors
- 🟢 184 unused resources found, an estimated $11.92 a month
- ⚠️ Compute Optimizer returned no idle recommendations; all 184 findings are types outside its list
- 🟢 $0.0026 per sweep, measured
